Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 October 2026

Tallymo is designed to help you track countdowns, habits and focus sessions while keeping your app records on your iPhone.

Information stored on your device

Countdown titles and dates, habit names and completion history, and focus timer information and preferences are stored locally on your device. Tallymo doesn't require a user account and the developer doesn't operate a server that receives these records.

Subscriptions

Optional Tallymo Pro subscriptions are handled by Apple through StoreKit. Apple processes payments and related transaction information under its own privacy terms. Tallymo checks your subscription status to unlock Pro features.

Notifications

If you enable timer alerts, the app schedules local notifications. You can manage notification permissions in your iPhone's Settings.

Analytics and advertising

The app doesn't include third-party analytics or advertising SDKs, and does not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

Data deletion and backups

You can delete individual countdowns and habits or use Delete All Data within the app to remove locally stored records. Uninstalling the app normally removes its local data. Existing device backups, if enabled, may retain copies under Apple's backup settings.

Changes

We may update this policy if the app's features or privacy practices change. Updates will be posted here with a new date.
