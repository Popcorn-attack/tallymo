Frequently asked questions

How do I add a countdown or habit?

Open the Countdown or Habits tab and tap the add button.

How do I restore Tallymo Pro?

Open the app's Settings screen and tap Restore Purchases. Use the same Apple Account that purchased Pro.

How do I manage or cancel my subscription?

Use Manage Subscription in Tallymo Settings or go to your iPhone's Settings, tap your Apple Account, then Subscriptions. Cancellation normally leaves access active until the end of the paid period.

How do I delete my records?

Delete individual countdowns and habits in the app or use the Delete All Data option in Settings.
